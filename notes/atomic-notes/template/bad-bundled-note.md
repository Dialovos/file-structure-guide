# Productivity thoughts

Some random reflections from this week. I've been thinking about how
context switching kills my morning, and I keep losing the first
fifteen minutes after every meeting. There's a real cost there that
I don't think people talk about enough.

Also, deep work needs longer blocks than I've been giving it. Two
hours minimum, ideally three. Anything shorter and I never get past
the warmup phase.

Single-tasking matters more than I admitted to myself last year.
Every time I try to "work on two things at once", both get done worse
and slower than if I had done them in series. I should test this
properly with a stopwatch.

Fatigue plays into this too — by 3pm my deep-work capacity is gone
and I'm fooling myself if I think I can grind through it. Better to
batch shallow work in the afternoon.

Meetings are still the dominant tax on my week. Even good meetings
cost an hour of recovery time afterward. Need to push back on the
3-meeting days.
