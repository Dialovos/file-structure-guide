Replace this with your real claim or content. Notes in a topic-driven vault are organised by what they're about; the file's location and name encode the topic.

## Why no date?

In topic-driven organisation, the *what* is the address. The note's modification time lives in file metadata, not in its name. If you find yourself wanting `2026-04-30-example-note.md`, you're starting to drift toward accidental mix.
