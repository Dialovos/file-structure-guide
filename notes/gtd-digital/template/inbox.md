# Inbox

Capture without thinking. Triage during the next inbox-clearing pass
(daily ideal, weekly minimum). Every item here gets:

1. Trashed, OR
2. Done in two minutes flat, OR
3. Promoted to a single line in `next-actions.md` (with a context), OR
4. Promoted to a project in `projects/` (if multi-step), OR
5. Filed in `reference/`, OR
6. Deferred to `someday-maybe.md` or `waiting-for.md`.

Nothing stays in this file. If it's been here a week, you're not
running GTD; you're running a wishlist.
