Open the current `Calendar/YYYY-Www.md` to review the week.

## How to use this MOC

1. Click any `[[+ Space]]` link above to drill into a space.
2. Daily capture goes in `Calendar/YYYY-MM-DD.md` — extract claims into `Notes/` over time.
3. New atomic notes land flat in `Notes/`. They get linked from one or more MOCs.
4. Add new spaces sparingly — most lives have 5-10 spaces, not 30.
