# Example claim as title

The filename is the claim. Notice how it asserts a position rather than naming a topic — a topic-titled note (`attention.md`) accumulates disjoint claims; a claim-titled note either holds up under scrutiny or gets split.

This note should fit on one screen. Outgoing links live inline:

- See [[another-declarative-title]] for a related claim.
- The supporting source extract lives in `literature/` (not in this folder).

Atomic, concept-oriented, declarative title, densely linked — the four evergreen rules. Replace this content with a real claim of your own.
