# About this garden

This is a digital garden — a public, slowly-tended collection of
notes that spans the maturity gradient from rough captures to
polished essays. Notes here aren't presented as finished blog posts;
they're presented as *thinking in motion*, with their current state
labeled.

## How this garden is organized

Every note carries one of three statuses:

- **Seedling** — a rough capture, often a few sentences or a
  half-formed thought. Don't expect polish; do expect honesty about
  where the idea is.
- **Budding** — an in-progress draft. Has structure, may have
  citations, isn't quite finished. The idea is taking shape but
  hasn't fully matured.
- **Evergreen** — refined, considered, ready-to-recommend. Still
  subject to revision as my thinking evolves; "evergreen" means
  *current polish*, not *frozen forever*.

The status appears at the top of every note. Browse the garden by
following links between notes, or start at the [home page](../INDEX.md)
which lists everything by status.

## Why a garden, not a blog

A blog rewards finished posts. A garden welcomes the half-formed.
That distinction matters because most of the value of public
writing — for me as the writer, and (I hope) for you as the reader —
lives in the messy middle, before an idea has earned a polished
finished post.

If you find a seedling here that excites you, write to me. Some of
my best evergreens started as a seedling someone else nudged me to
develop.

## How notes evolve

I tend the garden in batches — usually weekly. A typical tending
session: skim recent seedlings, promote the ripe ones to budding,
prune dead ones, refine a budding into an evergreen if it's ready.
The tending is the practice; the publishing is a side effect.

## Tools

This garden is published with a static-site generator that consumes
the markdown files in `seedlings/`, `budding/`, and `evergreen/`
directly. Backlinks are computed automatically from the `[[wikilinks]]`
in each note. The home page (`INDEX.md`) lists notes by status.
