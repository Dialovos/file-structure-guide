# A thought on attention residue

Sophie Leroy's "attention residue" — the idea that switching from
task A to task B leaves cognitive traces of A still active in your
head, blunting performance on B — has been knocking around in my
head this week.

What I want to figure out: is the residue worse when you finish A
incompletely, or when you've just spent a long time on it? Leroy's
original work suggested incompleteness was the bigger driver. If
that's right, finishing things — even small things — buys back
disproportionate cognitive bandwidth.

Half-formed thought. Will revisit. See also [[deep work]] and the
older idea of [[zeigarnik effect]].
