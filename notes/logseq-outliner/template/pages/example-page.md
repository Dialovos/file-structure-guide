- # Deep work
- ## What it is
	- Cal Newport's term: cognitively demanding work that progresses your skills, performed in a state of distraction-free concentration.
	- Contrasted with [[shallow-work]] — logistical, low-cognitive-load tasks that often feel productive but don't move skill or output forward.
- ## Why blocks matter
	- The minimum useful block is ~90 minutes. Below that, [[attention]] residue from previous tasks dominates the first chunk.
	- The maximum sustainable block (per Newport's research) is ~4 hours/day for most knowledge workers.
- ## Mechanisms
	- Single-tasking — no email, no Slack.
	- Closed-doors signaling — physical or virtual.
	- Pre-block ritual — clearing inputs, queueing the next deliverable.
- ## Open questions
	- What's the recovery time between two deep blocks in a single day?
	- Does the block-quality decay across the week?
